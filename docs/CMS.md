# CMS & ADMIN MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (v5.0)

## Overview
The ADFP CMS functions as a full-featured editorial content management dashboard:
- **Social Approval Queue**: Review AI-extracted posts from Instagram & Telegram before publishing.
- **Review Editor**: Multilingual editor with automatic Amharic translation suggestions and itemized ETB price tables.
- **Media Library**: Integrated Cloudinary media manager for high-res images and 9:16 vertical video reels.
- **Event Calendar**: Manage festival schedules, ticket allocations, and sponsor collateral.
