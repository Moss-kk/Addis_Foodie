# EDITORIAL GUIDELINES & INSPECTION CODE (v5.0)

## The 4-Step Inspection Methodology
1. **Anonymous Field Visits**: Inspectors dine unannounced as paying customers.
2. **Itemized ETB Price Receipt Audits**: All dish prices recorded in Ethiopian Birr from official receipts.
3. **Cinematic Food Photography & Video Reels**: Capturing authentic dish sizzle, roasting, and dining ambiance.
4. **Independent Verdict**: Ratings published independently with zero paid rating manipulation.
