# ADDIS FOODIES HANDOFF DOCUMENTATION (v4.0)

## Overview
Addis Foodies v4.0 implements the **Flame Design System** (`#120907` background, `#1A100C` card surfaces, `#E53935` Ember Red, `#FF8C00` Culinary Gold) and a **simplified 5-page routing structure**.

## Key Components & Pages
- **Homepage (`/`)**: Hero Search + 9:16 Video Reels (`VideoReelsCarousel.tsx`) + Top 4 Curated Spots + AI Craving Finder.
- **Reviews Archive (`/reviews`)**: Dedicated hub for full 50+ review grid, neighborhood filters, and price ETB selectors.
- **About (`/about`)**: Editorial 4-step inspection process, 150k+ reach stats, and brand story.
- **Events (`/events`)**: Kitfo Fest #5 and festival takeovers.
- **Commercial (`/services` & `/collaborate`)**: Restaurant onboarding & video reel production.

## Verification & Build
Verified via `cmd /c npm run build`. All 16 static routes compile cleanly.
